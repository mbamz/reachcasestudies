Breath mints & oral care

Every dollar came from somebody else's camera.

01The starting point$222 on 19 Jan 2026

Most “from zero” claims are unfalsifiable. This one has a date on it. On 19 January 2026 the brand TikTok Shop turned over $222.10. That is the baseline, and everything below is measured from it.

01

A shop doing $222/day. Live, but effectively dormant — no roster, no content velocity, no volume.

02

Two SKUs, full stop. No range to hide behind and no long tail to carry a bad month.

03

A considered-purchase price point. At ~$25 a unit, this is not an impulse category on a feed built for impulse.

04

Zero brand-posted content. Nobody at the brand was going to film their way out of this.

02The buildCreator engine · 4 phases

With two SKUs and no in-house content, there was only one lever worth pulling: build a creator roster big enough to test hooks at volume, then let the winners run.

Phase 1 · Foundation

Make two SKUs worth selling

  • Shop, catalog and fulfilment rebuilt around a 2-product range
  • Hero SKU — Sugar-Free 0-Calorie — positioned as the volume driver at $24.73
  • The flagship mint SKU 3X held as the $26.77 trade-up
A catalog with nowhere to hide
Phase 2 · Affiliate engine

Recruit the demand

  • Scaled to 561 active affiliates from a standing start
  • 956 creator videos in 30 days — 31 a day, continuously
  • Sampling, tiered commissions and briefs built so the roster produces unsupervised
$266/month of GMV per creator
Phase 3 · Compounding

Let the winners run

  • Daily rate went $1,308 → $4,976 — 3.8× — between the first 150 days and the last 30
  • The most recent month alone did 43% of all six-month revenue
  • Nothing was switched off to achieve it; the back catalog of videos kept selling
The curve steepens instead of flattening
Phase 4 · Amazon

Recapture the demand where it converts

  • Creator awareness lifting branded search and new-to-brand discovery on Amazon
  • ~50% halo lift across the same two-SKU catalog
  • +$1M added to Amazon growth across the same six months
One shopper, monetized twice
03Who actually posted the revenue100% creator · $0.00 brand

Most brands post something. The brand posted nothing at all. Of $149.28K in TikTok Shop GMV over 30 days, self-operated account revenue is $0.00 — not rounded down, not negligible, zero. Creators posted $149,250. It is the cleanest possible proof that the roster is the channel.

04The results$149K TikTok · +$1M Amazon

The shape matters as much as the number. Six months of revenue is $345.47K — and 43% of it landed in the last 30 days. This is a curve that is still steepening.

06What it proves3 takeaways

Two SKUs and zero brand-posted content produced a $1.79M run rate. The asset was never the range — it was 561 creators.

Price point is an excuse, not a constraint$24.76 average unit price on a feed everyone insists only sells $10 impulse buys. Enough creators will find the hook that makes it make sense.
