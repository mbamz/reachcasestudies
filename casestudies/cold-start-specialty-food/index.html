01The starting pointCult brand · no shop

The brand is one of the most recognizable names in food — and none of that equity was converting on the channel where food goes viral. The products were built for creator content; there was just no shoppable path pointing at them.

01

No TikTok Shop presence. Cult products, devoted fanbase, and nowhere to buy in-app.

02

No creator engine. Zero affiliates enrolled, zero shoppable content in circulation.

03

Highly filmable products. Chili crunch and noodles are made for unboxings and recipes — untapped.

02The buildCreator engine · 3 phases

Take products people already love, put them in front of creators who cook and unbox, and give the fanbase a place to buy. The roster does the selling; the pantry staples do the converting.

Phase 1 · Launch

Shop & pantry staples

  • TikTok Shop stood up from zero: catalog, offers, fulfilment
  • Led with the cult heroes — chili crunch, soy & scallion noodles
A shoppable path for an existing fanbase
Phase 2 · Creator engine

Seed the roster

  • Matched food and lifestyle creators, with sampling, briefs and viral hooks
  • 890 creator videos in 90 days — unboxings, recipes and taste tests
  • Measurable growth in sales and reach within two weeks
Pantry staples turned into viral content
Phase 3 · Amplify

Fuel the winners

  • Paid amplification behind the breakout creator videos
  • Scaled reach and sell-through on the content already working
$960K in the first 90 days
03The results$0 → $960K in 90 days

890 videos at roughly $1,079 each is what a matched creator roster does with a product that films itself. The fanbase was always there — the launch just gave it a shoppable path and a reason to hit “add to cart.”
