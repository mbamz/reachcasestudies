01The three-channel loopTikTok → Amazon → DSP

Most brands run one channel, or two that ignore each other. The brand runs three, wired in sequence — and it is the clearest picture of what the engine actually does: create demand where nobody is shopping, convert it where everybody is, then recapture whoever slipped through.

02The starting point$352K prior year

The brand sells organic sleep and pregnancy wellness — magnesium lotion for people who cannot sleep, at fifty-four dollars a unit. It is the hardest possible brief for a short-form feed: a considered, trust-dependent purchase in a category full of claims, sold to someone who has already tried four things that didn't work.

01

01~$352K in the prior-year window. A working channel, but nowhere near its ceiling.

02

A $54 average unit price. Five times what conventional wisdom says a short-form feed will bear.

03

A trust purchase, not an impulse one. Nobody buys a sleep remedy because a video was fast — they buy because someone credible explained it.

04

Amazon demand left uncaptured. Creator awareness was landing, and browsers who didn't convert were walking away unretargeted.

03The build4 phases · 3 channels

The sequence matters. Creators create demand, Amazon converts it, DSP recaptures the leak, and the whole thing is measured on one blended number rather than three competing dashboards.

Phase 1 · Creator engine

Sell a $54 product with other people's credibility

  • Affiliate revenue $852.57K of $861.32K — 98.98% of everything
  • Brand's own account posted $0.00 across the entire six months
  • 15.94K units at $54.05 — 88.5 units a day at a considered price point
+145% year over year
Phase 2 · Amazon

Convert where the intent lands

  • Creator awareness lifting branded search and new-to-brand discovery
  • ~22% halo lift across the catalog
  • Listings and ad structure built to catch demand the feed created
Demand arrives pre-sold
Phase 3 · Amazon DSP

Recapture the 90% who browse and leave

  • Retargeting built specifically against creator-driven traffic
  • +35% lift in new-customer retargeting
  • The audience TikTok warmed up gets a second and third chance to convert
The leak becomes a channel
Phase 4 · Compounding

One number, not three

  • $861.32K TikTok Shop + $1.3M Amazon = $2.16M in six months
  • Blended TACOS measured across all three channels, not per silo
  • Every creator video stays live and keeps feeding the loop
$2.16M across six months
04Who actually posted the revenue98.98% affiliate · $0.00 brand

Six months, $861.32K, and the brand did not post once. Of $861.32K over 180 days, self-operated account revenue is $0.00. Creators posted $852.57K. Shopping Mall placement contributed $8.76K — one percent.

05The results$2.16M across 3 channels

TikTok Shop banked $861.32K. Amazon banked $1.3M it would not otherwise have seen. DSP made the Amazon half 35% more efficient at buying new customers. Score TikTok Shop on its own P&L and you would call this a $861K channel. Score the engine, and it is $2.16M — with the cheapest new-customer acquisition of the three.
